## Highlights & Concepts Covered

* **Entity-Relationship Modeling:** Designing robust ER diagrams tailored for real-world scenarios (e.g., Indian E-Commerce platforms).
* **SQL & Relational Algebra:** Executing data definition, data manipulation, complex multi-table joins, and aggregate operations.
* **Procedural SQL:** Implementing PL/SQL blocks, cursors, stored procedures, and triggers for automated database logic.
* **Schema Design & Normalization:** Decomposing relations to eliminate anomalies and ensure optimal functional dependencies.
* **Transaction Control:** Managing ACID properties, locks, and recovery techniques.
